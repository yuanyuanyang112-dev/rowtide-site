Rowtide Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

Rowtide is a row counter and project notebook for knitting and crochet. We built it to work without collecting any of your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Rowtide has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Your projects, counters, yarn details, notes, pattern photos you pick, and app settings are stored only on your iPhone (inside the app and its Home Screen widget). We cannot see this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos

When you add a pattern photo, you choose it with the system photo picker. Rowtide only gets the photos you select and keeps a compressed copy on your device.

Export

If you choose Export, Rowtide creates a file and saves it where you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

Purchases

Rowtide Pro is purchased through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy, and we don’t receive your payment details.

Children

Rowtide does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before the change ships.
